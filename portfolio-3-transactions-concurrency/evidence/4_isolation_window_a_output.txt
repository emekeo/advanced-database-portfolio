=== ISOLATION, WINDOW A: billing officer checks how much has been paid on INV-2026-000003, twice in one transaction
Start 4_isolation_window_b.sql in a second window, then follow the steps.

##### PART 1: READ COMMITTED (the PostgreSQL default)
Once window B is open, press Enter here for step 1: BEGIN ISOLATION LEVEL READ COMMITTED;
BEGIN
SELECT SUM(payment.amount) AS amount_paid
FROM payment
JOIN invoice ON invoice.invoice_id = payment.invoice_id
WHERE invoice.invoice_no = 'INV-2026-000003';
 amount_paid 
-------------
     8000.00
(1 row)


>>> Next: step 2 in window B
Do step 2 in window B first, then press Enter here for step 3: SELECT SUM(payment.amount) AS amount_paid
FROM payment
JOIN invoice ON invoice.invoice_id = payment.invoice_id

 amount_paid

     9000.00
(1 row)

COMMIT;
COMMIT
Same query, same transaction, different answer: a non-repeatable read.
