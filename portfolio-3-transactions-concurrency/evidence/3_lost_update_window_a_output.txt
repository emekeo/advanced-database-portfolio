=== LOST UPDATE, WINDOW A: pharmacist Emeka Ani dispenses 10 Salbutamol 4mg tablets
Start 3_lost_update_window_b.sql in a second window, then follow the steps.

##### PART 1: read the stock, work out the new figure, write it back (no lock)
Once window B is open, press Enter here for step 1: BEGIN;
BEGIN
SELECT medicine.quantity_on_hand AS stock_seen
FROM medicine
WHERE medicine.name = 'Salbutamol' AND medicine.form = 'tablet' 
Window A sees 40 tablets in stock

>>> Next: step 2 in window B
Do step 2 in window B first, then press Enter here for step 3: UPDATE medicine
SET quantity_on_hand = 40 - 10
WHERE medicine.name = 'Salbutamol' AND medicine.form = 'tablet';
UPDATE 1
COMMIT;
COMMIT
Window A saved 40 - 10 and committed

>>> Next: step 4 in window B
Do step 4 in window B first, then press Enter here for step 5: 
##### PART 2: the same, but the read locks the row with SELECT ... FOR UPDATE
BEGIN;
BEGIN
SELECT medicine.quantity_on_hand AS stock_seen
FROM medicine
WHERE medicine.name = 'Salbutamol' AND medicine.form = 'tablet'
FOR UPDATE 
Window A sees 40 tablets in stock and holds a lock on the row

>>> Next: step 6 in window B. Window B will wait.
Do step 6 in window B first (it will wait), then press Enter here for step 7: UPDATE medicine
SET quantity_on_hand = 40 - 10
WHERE medicine.name = 'Salbutamol' AND medicine.form = 'tablet';
UPDATE 1
COMMIT;
COMMIT
Window A saved 40 - 10, committed and released the lock

>>> Next: look at window B, which has now carried on by itself
